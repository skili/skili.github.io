Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 October 2026

This policy explains what the Blooku app ("Blooku", "we") collects, why, and what you can do about it. Questions: ineifepaul@gmail.com.

1. What we collect

  • From your Google account, when you sign in: your name, email address and profile photo. We do not get your Google password, contacts, Gmail or Drive.
  • What you add to your profile: display name, username, bio and profile photo.
  • What you post: photos, videos and text, captions, event names, comments and replies, and the groups you post in.
  • What you do in the app: likes, which posts you viewed and when, who you follow, who you block, the groups you join or create, join requests, group mutes, and reports you send.
  • Notifications: the notifications made for you, and a push token from your phone so we can send alerts. You can switch each kind of alert off in Settings.
  • Activity time: the time you were last active in the app (one time per account).

During sign-up we ask your birthday only to check that you are 16 or older. It is never saved or shown.

We do not collect your location, your contacts, or advertising IDs. Blooku has no ads.

2. Who can see what

  • Your username, name, photo, bio and follower counts are visible to other users.
  • Your posts are visible to your followers, to members of the groups you post them in, and, for posts made in no group or in a public group, to other Blooku users. Posts in a private group are visible only to its members.
  • Anyone who can see a post can see its likes, comments and who viewed it.
  • If you block someone, neither of you sees the other's posts or activity.

3. How we use it

Only to run Blooku: show your profile and posts, build your feeds, deliver notifications and alerts, keep accounts safe, and handle reports and rule breaking. We do not sell your data or share it for advertising.

4. Services we use

  • Supabase stores your account, posts, photos and videos, and runs our server.
  • Google Sign-In signs you in.
  • Firebase Cloud Messaging (Google) delivers push alerts to Android phones. It receives only the alert text and, where there is one, the profile photo of the person who did the action. It stores no other Blooku data.

5. How long we keep it

  • A post made with "Expires in" (24 hours, 48 hours or 7 days) is deleted when that time is up, with its likes, comments, views and media. A post with an open report may be kept until the report is handled.
  • Other posts stay until you delete them.
  • When you delete your account (Settings → Account options → Delete account), it is hidden from everyone at once. If you sign in again within 90 days, it comes back. After 90 days your account and everything you made are deleted for good.

6. Your choices

  • Edit or remove your profile details and photo at any time.
  • Delete any of your posts and comments.
  • Turn push alerts on or off in Settings.
  • Delete your account as described above, or email us to ask for a copy or deletion of your data.

7. Age

Blooku is for people aged 16 and over.

8. Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date above.
